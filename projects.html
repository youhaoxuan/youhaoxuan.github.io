High volume · Current · Tesla

LFP cell & module assembly line

Own the LFP cell and module stack assembly processes for 24/7 high-volume production at Tesla, with 15% cycle-time reduction and process capability held above Cpk 1.67. Drive yield across laser, ultrasonic and hot-melt welding and leak detection through structured root-cause analysis, lifting line rolled yield 10%. Configure MES data collection with in-line SPC and build dashboards tracking failures by fixture, track and batch.

High volume · Tesla

Busbar laser-weld scrap reduction

Cut busbar laser-weld scrap by 80% through DOE and laboratory characterization of the weld. Deployed in-line vision inspection for busbar position accuracy and replaced a manual cleaning step with an automated process, improving weld quality while cutting both cycle time and operator labor.

High volume · Tesla

Station commissioning & line ramp

Led commissioning, qualification and ramp of seven new cell assembly stations with domestic and international OEM suppliers: process flow mapping, equipment function definition, fixture design improvement and validation, mechanical checks, dry and wet runs, PFMEA, GR&R, error proofing, MES configuration, control plans and SOPs, through OEE demonstration, buy-off and integration into the mass-production line. Throughput ramped from hundreds to tens of thousands of cells per day; the module stack line from 20% to 80% of design capacity at stable output.

High volume · Tesla

Top-cap weld fixture redesign

Replaced consumable PEEK tape on the top-cap laser welding fixture with a screw-fastened PEEK block, eliminating the tape placement variation that was driving weld quality variation and yield loss, and extending the replacement interval from every 3 days to every 2 weeks. Changeover time and unplanned downtime both fell.

SPC & validation data pipeline

Built Python and JMP tooling to automate data collection, visualization and database generation, then MES-fed SPC and OEE dashboards with automated Cpk trend reporting, alerting process and maintenance teams to drift early enough to correct before NG product was produced, worth roughly 2% yield per station.
